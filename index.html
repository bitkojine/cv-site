This site documents active customer research and cold outreach experiments.

Team method: define one narrow customer segment, write a structured brief, send 100 direct messages, book 20-minute customer interviews from replies, and test assumptions against real constraints before changing positioning.

We run five customer tracks in parallel. Five co-founders each own one customer brief and commit to 100 outreach messages in that segment. This is not volume for its own sake. The goal is to convert replies into real conversations and learn what customers actually care about.

Available Briefs

  • B2B SaaS Head of Sales
  • E-commerce Director of Retention
  • Multi-Location Health Clinic Operations Manager
  • Regional Logistics Company Owner-Operator
  • Recruiting Agency Founder

How to Use This

  1. Pick one brief per co-founder.
  2. DM 100 people matching that profile.
  3. Book 20-minute interviews from the people who reply.
  4. Use the brief questions during calls and log exact responses.
  5. Track objections, no-replies, and rejection language.
  6. Extract patterns after the first 100 messages and interview outcomes.
  7. Update the brief using what rejection taught you.
