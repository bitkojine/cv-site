This site documents active customer research and interview-led outreach experiments.

A brief is a working document for one customer segment. It defines who we are trying to understand, what pain we believe they have, what angles we will test in outreach, and what interview questions we will ask on 20-minute calls.

Goal: avoid building internal theories without contact with reality. We send 100 messages, convert a subset into real conversations, test ideas with real people, and learn from rejection. Pick the customer segment you actually want to work with and test it directly.

How to Use This

  1. Pick one vertical you want to serve.
  2. Send 100 targeted outreach messages in that segment.
  3. Book 20-minute interviews from replies.
  4. Use the brief questions, and capture exact wording.
  5. Track reply rate, call conversion, objections, and hard rejections.
  6. Update the brief based on evidence, not internal assumptions.
