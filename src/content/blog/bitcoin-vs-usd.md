# Why I Prefer Bitcoin Over USD (A Personal Story)

I grew up watching prices move faster than wages. Every year a little more of everyday life felt out of reach. It was not dramatic, just a steady leak. That is the backdrop for why I started looking for alternatives to holding most of my savings in cash.

I did not start with a grand ideology. I started with a question: how do I store value over a decade without needing to guess which institutions will be careful and which will be reckless?

## What Bitcoin Gave Me

Bitcoin gave me a different set of tradeoffs. It is volatile, still young, and not for everyone. But it also gave me three things the dollar does not:

- a fixed supply policy I can verify
- custody I can control
- the ability to move value across borders without asking for permission

Those three features changed the way I think about long‑term savings.

## What the Dollar Still Does Well

USD is still the best tool I have for day‑to‑day life. Taxes, rent, groceries, short‑term budgeting, and stable unit‑of‑account pricing all work better with dollars. I am not trying to replace that. I am trying to complement it.

## Why I Tilt Toward Bitcoin

For me, the decision is less about “Bitcoin versus USD” and more about time horizon. I hold dollars for spending and liquidity. I hold Bitcoin for long‑term optionality and the ability to self‑custody.

That choice is personal. It depends on your risk tolerance, your time horizon, and your comfort with new technology. If you cannot sleep with volatility, do not force it. I prefer the tradeoff because I want a savings asset that is global, portable, and hard to dilute.

## A Simple Framework

Here is the framework I use today:

- dollars for short‑term needs and stability
- bitcoin for long‑term savings I do not plan to touch soon

I do not see this as a moral stance. It is just how I balance utility and sovereignty in my own life.

Nothing in this post is financial advice. It is a personal story about how I think and why I made the choices I did.
